The challenge
with most agents

Complicated setup
Local first
Connector limitations
Constant monitoring
Painful sharing
Grok Bot flips every one of these.
Talk to it

No config files. Tell a bot its job in plain English, like a new teammate.

Its own computer

Runs in the cloud, 24/7. Nothing to install, nothing tied to your laptop.

Logs into your tools

Gmail, Slack, Canvas, Sheets, the browser. It uses them like you would.

Works unattended

Routines and automations. It reports back; you do not babysit it.

Share it like a link

Hand your bot to a friend or your whole club in one click.
