Yale AIAn Undergraduate OrganizationOur mission

Learn. Build. Collaborate.

01 · Learn

Start now. Start here.

No experience? No problem. Yale AI welcomes students of all majors and skill levels. A semester-long educational fellowship and workshops to introduce you to the AI ecosystem: agents, AI-native workflows, mini hackathons.

02 · Build

Build real projects.

Join a project team: Yale AI × Medicine, × Finance, × Yale. Or propose your own idea. Every team gets an industry or institutional partner, produces a real product, and presents at the end-of-term demo day.

03 · Collaborate

Connect with others.

We get you in the rooms that matter: top companies and AI labs in NYC and Boston, AI-adjacent labs on campus, fireside chats with founders and researchers, the Yale AI Symposium, and club events with fellow Yalies.
